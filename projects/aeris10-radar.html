Crawler

Rapid Deployment Radar System — an open-source, low-cost 10.5 GHz phased array radar system being developed for surveillance, reconnaissance, and situational awareness applications.

Project Overview

Crawler is an open-source, low-cost 10.5 GHz phased array radar system under active development. It features Pulse Linear Frequency Modulated (LFM) modulation and is designed for rapid deployment in field operations, border surveillance, drone detection, and situational awareness applications.

🚧 Work in Progress

This project is currently in the design and prototyping phase. The system architecture is being finalized, and component selection is underway.

Project Goals

  • Rapid Deployment Capability Design a radar system that can be deployed quickly in the field with minimal setup time.
  • Open Source & Accessible Provide complete schematics, PCB layouts, firmware, and software to democratize radar technology.
  • Modular Architecture Separate power management, frequency synthesis, and RF boards for easier development and maintenance.
  • Advanced Signal Processing Implement pulse compression, Doppler processing, MTI, and CFAR on onboard FPGA.
  • Affordable & Scalable Keep costs low while providing a platform that can scale from short-range to long-range applications.

Planned Applications

  • Border & Perimeter Surveillance Rapid deployment for monitoring borders, critical infrastructure, and restricted areas.
  • Drone Detection & Tracking Detect and track unauthorized UAVs in sensitive airspace.
  • Search & Rescue Operations Locate and track personnel or vessels in emergency situations.
  • Research & Education Platform for universities and researchers to experiment with phased array radar technology.
