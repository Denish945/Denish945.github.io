Who I Am

I am an Aeronautical Engineering graduate with a deep passion for aircraft design, engineering systems, and aviation technology. My journey has taken me from the fundamentals of aerodynamics and aircraft performance to the practical world of aircraft maintenance, and beyond into software development, automation, and electronics.

I believe in the intersection of physical engineering and digital systems. Whether I'm designing a UAV, developing an aviation management system, or programming a PLC, I approach every project with the same engineering mindset: understand the problem, design a solution, and build something that works.

My Engineering Journey

My academic training covered the full spectrum of aeronautical engineering — from aircraft design and aerodynamic analysis to manufacturing, modelling, and engineering development. I currently work in aircraft maintenance, which gives me invaluable practical exposure to the operational and maintenance side of aviation.

Alongside aeronautical engineering, I have developed expertise in software engineering, building full-stack applications, automation systems, and electronics. This combination allows me to see engineering problems from multiple perspectives and develop integrated solutions that bridge the gap between hardware and software.

My Engineering Philosophy

"I like building things. My interests sit between physical engineering and digital systems."

I believe the best engineers understand both the physical and digital worlds. Whether it's designing an aircraft wing, developing a radar system, or building a software platform, the underlying principles are the same: understand the problem, design a solution, and build it well.

I enjoy taking an engineering problem, understanding the underlying physics and constraints, developing a design, and then using software and modern engineering tools to analyse, automate, or improve the solution. This integrated approach allows me to tackle complex challenges that require both technical depth and systems thinking.
