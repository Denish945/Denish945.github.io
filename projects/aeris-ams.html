AERIS AMS

Aviation Engineering & Management Information System — a full-stack platform designed to digitize operational, maintenance, and engineering workflows in aviation organizations.

Status Testing & Evaluation
Timeline Jun 2025 - Jul 2026
Role Lead Developer
Stack React · Flask · PostgreSQL

System Overview

AERIS AMS is a comprehensive aviation management platform designed to streamline operations in aircraft maintenance, engineering, and fleet management. The system integrates multiple modules to provide a unified interface for managing maintenance schedules, inventory, personnel, equipment, financials, and documentation.

Purpose & Vision

The aviation industry relies on complex workflows and strict regulatory compliance. AERIS AMS was developed to bridge the gap between engineering operations and digital management, providing a single source of truth for all aviation-related activities. Built with PostgreSQL for robust data integrity and scalability.

Key Features

  • Stores & Inventory Management Comprehensive inventory tracking with automated reordering, supplier management, and usage analytics.
  • Continued Airworthiness Tracking Monitor and track aircraft airworthiness status, compliance records, and regulatory documentation.
  • GSE & Tools Management Manage Ground Support Equipment and tools with calibration tracking, maintenance scheduling, and availability status.
  • Work Order Management Create, assign, and track work orders with priority levels, status updates, and completion verification.
  • Aircrafts Module Maintain comprehensive aircraft records including registration, maintenance history, modifications, and operational status.
  • Document Control Centralized document storage with version control, approval workflows, and regulatory compliance tracking.
  • HR & Personnel Management Staff records, certification tracking, shift scheduling, leave management, and performance metrics.

Design Principles

Modularity

Each module is independent and can be deployed separately, enabling flexibility and scalability. The system is highly customizable to fit different aviation operation sizes.

Security

Role-based access control, encrypted data, and secure authentication protect sensitive aviation data and ensure regulatory compliance.

Scalability

Designed to handle growing data volumes and increasing user loads without performance degradation, powered by PostgreSQL's robust architecture.

Usability

Intuitive interface designed for aviation professionals, minimizing training requirements and streamlining daily operations.
