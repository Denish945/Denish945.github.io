Fixed-Wing Surveillance Drone (Asopy Aircraft Design)

Conceptual development of a long-endurance, locally manufacturable fixed-wing UAV for surveillance missions. Currently in the preliminary design phase, with regulatory classification as a Category C drone under Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) regulations.

Status Preliminary Design
Phase Concept & Sizing
KCAA Category C (>25 kg)
Timeline 2025 – Present

Project Scope

This project aims to design a fixed-wing surveillance drone capable of extended endurance missions, with a focus on local manufacturability and regulatory compliance. The design process follows a structured engineering lifecycle, currently at the preliminary design stage.

The drone is intended for surveillance, reconnaissance, and monitoring applications, and is being developed with the goal of eventual production using locally available materials and processes.

Regulatory Classification – KCAA Category C

Under the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority (KCAA) regulations for unmanned aircraft systems, this drone falls under Category C, which covers UAVs with a maximum take-off mass greater than 25 kg. This classification imposes specific operational and certification requirements, including:

  • Registration and licensing with KCAA
  • Certification of airworthiness
  • Pilot licensing and training
  • Operational limitations (altitude, line-of-sight, etc.)
  • Safety and risk management protocols

The design is being developed with these regulatory requirements in mind, ensuring that the final aircraft can be certified and operated legally within Kenya and compliant regions.

Design Lifecycle

The project follows a phased engineering approach:

  • Boundary Selection – Defining mission requirements, constraints, and design parameters.
  • Conceptual Design – Configuration selection, initial sizing, and trade-off studies.
  • Preliminary Design – Detailed aerodynamic analysis, structural layout, propulsion integration, and performance estimation (current phase).
  • Detailed Design – Complete CAD modeling, structural analysis, systems integration.
  • Prototyping – Fabrication of a proof-of-concept prototype.
  • Testing & Evaluation – Flight testing, performance validation, and refinement.

Local Manufacturing & Regulatory Alignment

🌍 Local Manufacturing

A core objective is to design the UAV using materials and processes that are available locally in Kenya. This reduces import dependency, lowers costs, and builds local aerospace capability. Current research focuses on:

  • Composite fabrication using locally sourced fibers and resins
  • CNC machining of metal components
  • 3D printing for complex parts
  • Modular assembly to simplify production

📋 KCAA Category C Compliance

The design is being developed in alignment with KCAA regulations for unmanned aircraft in Category C. Key considerations include:

  • Structural integrity for certification
  • Safety systems (redundancy, fail-safe)
  • Operational documentation
  • Pilot training and licensing framework
  • Maintenance and airworthiness procedures
