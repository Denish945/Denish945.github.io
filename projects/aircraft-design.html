Aircraft Design

Conceptual UAV development focused on aerodynamic optimization, structural integrity, and mission-specific performance.

Status Completed
Year 2023
Role Lead Designer
Tools SolidWorks, CFD, MATLAB

Overview

This project involved the conceptual design of a UAV optimized for endurance and payload capacity. The design process encompassed aerodynamic analysis, structural modeling, propulsion system selection, and performance simulation.

Key Objectives

  • Design an airframe with an optimal lift-to-drag ratio for extended flight duration.
  • Select appropriate materials and structural configuration for durability and weight efficiency.
  • Integrate a propulsion system that balances power output with fuel efficiency.
  • Validate design performance through computational simulations.

Design Process

01

Conceptual Design

Initial mission requirements analysis, configuration selection, and preliminary sizing calculations.

02

Aerodynamic Analysis

CFD simulations to optimize airfoil selection, wing planform, and control surface design.

03

Structural Design

Detailed structural modeling, material selection, and stress analysis for all major components.

04

Performance Validation

Flight performance simulations, stability analysis, and mission profile validation.
