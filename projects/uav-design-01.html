Long-Range Fixed-Wing UAV

Designing the next generation of locally manufacturable unmanned aircraft for long-range surveillance and endurance missions.

Status Conceptual Design
Year 2024 – Present
Role Lead Designer
Tools SolidWorks, CFD, MATLAB

Project Overview

This project involves the conceptual design of a long-range fixed-wing UAV optimized for surveillance, endurance, and local manufacturability. The design process encompasses aerodynamic analysis, structural modeling, propulsion system selection, and performance simulation.

The central ambition of this project is to explore how unmanned aircraft can be designed, developed, and eventually produced locally—contributing to the growth of local aerospace capability while meeting demanding mission requirements.

Design Objective

  • Design a fixed-wing UAV with optimal aerodynamic efficiency for extended flight duration
  • Select appropriate materials and structural configuration for durability and manufacturability
  • Integrate a propulsion system that balances power output with fuel/energy efficiency
  • Validate design performance through computational simulations
  • Ensure manufacturability using locally available materials and processes
  • Meet long-range surveillance mission requirements

Mission Requirements

  • Extended range for long-duration surveillance missions
  • Payload capacity for sensors and surveillance equipment
  • Operational flexibility in diverse environmental conditions
  • Reliable communication and control systems
  • Cost-effective manufacturing and maintenance
  • Scalable production capability

Design Process

01

Conceptual Design

Initial mission requirements analysis, configuration selection, and preliminary sizing calculations.

02

Aerodynamic Analysis

CFD simulations to optimize airfoil selection, wing planform, and control surface design for maximum efficiency.

03

Structural Design

Detailed structural modeling, material selection, and stress analysis for all major components.

04

Propulsion & Systems

Propulsion system selection, integration, and avionics/communication systems design.

05

Performance Validation

Flight performance simulations, stability analysis, and mission profile validation.

06

Manufacturing Approach

Design for manufacturability using locally available materials and production processes.

Local Manufacturing Approach

🏭

Materials Selection

Sourcing materials that balance performance with local availability, reducing dependency on specialized imports and enabling sustainable production.

🔧

Production Processes

Designing for manufacturability using accessible production methods—composite fabrication, CNC machining, and 3D printing where appropriate.

📐

Design for Assembly

Modular design approach that simplifies assembly, maintenance, and repair while reducing production complexity and cost.

🌍

Local Capability Development

Contributing to the development of local aerospace manufacturing capability and technical expertise in unmanned aircraft production.
