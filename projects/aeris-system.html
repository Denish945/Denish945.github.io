AERIS

Aviation Engineering & Management Information System — a full-stack platform designed to digitize operational, maintenance, and engineering workflows in aviation.

Status Active Development
Year 2024-2026
Role Lead Developer
Stack React · Flask · MongoDB

System Overview

AERIS is a comprehensive aviation management platform designed to streamline operations in aircraft maintenance, engineering, and fleet management. The system integrates multiple modules to provide a unified interface for managing maintenance schedules, inventory, personnel, equipment, financials, and documentation.

Purpose & Vision

The aviation industry relies on complex workflows and strict regulatory compliance. AERIS was developed to bridge the gap between engineering operations and digital management, providing a single source of truth for all aviation-related activities.

Key Features

  • Maintenance Management Track and schedule maintenance tasks, log inspections, and manage compliance records.
  • Inventory Control Monitor parts inventory, track usage, and manage supply chain logistics.
  • Equipment Tracking Manage fleet records, equipment history, and operational status.
  • Personnel Management Staff scheduling, certification tracking, and team coordination.
  • Financial Management Cost tracking, budget management, and financial reporting.
  • Workflow Automation Automated task assignment, approval workflows, and process management.
  • Documentation Centralized document storage, version control, and regulatory compliance.

Design Principles

Modularity

Each module is independent and can be deployed separately, enabling flexibility and scalability.

Security

Role-based access control, encrypted data, and secure authentication protect sensitive aviation data.

Scalability

Designed to handle growing data volumes and increasing user loads without performance degradation.

Usability

Intuitive interface designed for aviation professionals, minimizing training requirements.
